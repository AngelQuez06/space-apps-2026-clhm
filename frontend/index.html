SOURCES AND LIMITATIONSGale terrain network · loadingView details

Map: stylized Gale planner, loading georeferenced sites and segments.

CLHM shows tiles from NASA Mars Trek, night infrared imagery from THEMIS, and slopes from a USGS HRSC+MOLA DEM at about 200 m per pixel. THEMIS shows nighttime brightness, not calibrated temperature; the DEM may miss small obstacles.

Routes and metrics use the terrain model. No live position or validated safety assessment is provided.
