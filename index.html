The Challenge

On Mars, every step costs
oxygen, time, and radiation.

<1%

Almost no shield

Mars has less than 1% of Earth's atmospheric pressure and no global magnetic field. Radiation reaches the crew directly.

Heavy suits

Two astronauts in pressurized suits plus a cargo rover. Every climb increases oxygen consumption.

Unforgiving terrain

Slopes over 15° stop the rover and over 20° block walking. Loose sand and fractured rock lie along the path.
